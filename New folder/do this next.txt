One module I'd add that isn't in most ERPs

I'd create a Resource Assignment module.

Instead of storing sales_rep_id and project_manager_id directly on a project, use a table like:

Project Assignment
------------------
Project
Employee
Role
Start Date
End Date
Primary?

Roles could include:

Sales Representative
Project Manager
Assistant PM
Estimator
Field Lead
Technician
Sample Collector
Safety Officer
Customer Contact

That solves the problem you described earlier: if a project manager is out sick or leaves the company, you can reassign all of their project assignments without changing your data model. It also lets one person hold multiple roles on the same project (for example, a salesperson who is also acting as the project manager).

Before adding all of these

I would not build all 19 phases in order. I would identify the workflows your team performs every day and make those excellent first.

Based on what you've shared, I'd prioritize:

CRM (Accounts, Contacts, Facilities, Job Sites)
Projects (including Project Assignments)
Scheduling & Dispatch
Field Operations (daily logs, issues, photos, materials)
Documents & SharePoint
Reporting
Mobile apps
AI

That sequence gets a usable platform into your team's hands much sooner while giving you room to refine later modules based on real usage.
